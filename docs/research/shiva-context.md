# Contextual Research — Shiva

## 1. Visual Context

Shiva has been visually reinterpreted throughout the Final Fantasy series.
Her appearance changes significantly between games, often reflecting the
role, personality and narrative proposed for that particular interpretation.

Despite these variations, some visual characteristics remain strongly
associated with Shiva, particularly her feminine appearance, pale blue skin
and connection to ice.

Her visual development across the series also reflects changes in how the
character is presented. Earlier interpretations often emphasized a more
sexually provocative appearance, while later versions tend to place greater
emphasis on character identity, narrative importance and visual symbolism.

### Personal Observations

The changes between different versions reinforce the idea that Shiva does
not depend on one fixed visual interpretation to remain recognizable.

For this project, this creates room to develop a new visual direction while
keeping recognizable characteristics from the FFXIV version as the main
anchor.

---

## 2. FFXIV Context

FFXIV presents Shiva through two visually distinct interpretations that
share several recognizable characteristics while using very different
design approaches.

### Original Shiva

The first version appears after Ysayle invokes Shiva and uses her body as a
vessel for the Primal.

The encounter takes place at the Akh Afah Amphitheatre, an environment
dominated by ice and rock.

Visually, this version has a more imposing and mature appearance. Her crown
is formed by long, sharp ice-like shapes that create a particularly strong
silhouette. Her cape is divided into four sections, with two longer pieces
ending in sharp, blade-like forms.

Her palette is heavily centered around pale blue and white, reinforcing the
connection to ice. The design also incorporates exposed areas of the body,
giving the character a more sensual appearance.

### Personal Observations

The first version communicates power very effectively. The combination of
the crown, cape and body proportions creates a recognizable silhouette even
from a distance.

The crown is particularly important because it can be interpreted both as
an ice formation and as a royal symbol. This contributes to the impression
of a powerful and almost regal figure.

The design also resembles the visual language commonly used for powerful
magical or divine figures in fantasy, which influenced my initial
interpretation of Shiva as distant, powerful and authoritative.

### Eden's Verse — Refulgence

The second version is a recreation of Shiva created within Eden's story.
Ryne becomes her avatar, and the resulting manifestation becomes a threat
that the player must confront.

The encounter takes place in an environment dominated by ice. The arena
contains large ice formations and a central pattern that reinforces the
visual theme.

This version changes Shiva's appearance significantly. Her crown is smaller
and more refined, creating a more delicate and youthful impression. Her
white hair and pale blue skin remain recognizable, while her clothing is
more elaborate and layered.

The design also gives her a more battle-oriented appearance, closer to a
magical warrior or battlemage.

### Personal Observations

Although both versions represent Shiva and share the same fundamental
visual identity, they feel surprisingly different.

The contrast between them demonstrates how much the character can change
while remaining recognizable. This is particularly relevant to the project
because it suggests that a strong reinterpretation does not necessarily
require reproducing the exact original design.

## 3. Recurring Visual Elements

Several visual elements remain strongly associated with Shiva:

- Pale blue and icy white
- Ice and crystal-like formations
- Sharp and angular shapes
- Long white hair
- Feminine silhouette
- Crown or crown-like elements
- Magical effects associated with ice
- Elegant and refined visual forms

### Personal Observations

The ice associated with Shiva is visually closer to magical crystal
formations than to completely natural ice.

The repeated use of sharp shapes, pointed forms and pale blue tones creates
a recognizable visual language that can be adapted into other elements of
the project.

The crown is particularly interesting because it connects two ideas that
appear repeatedly in my interpretation of Shiva: **ice and nobility**.

---

## 4. Environment & Atmosphere

The environments associated with Shiva reinforce the same visual language
found in her character design.

Ice, rock, crystalline formations and cold lighting create spaces that feel
large, isolated and powerful.

### Personal Observations

The environments reinforce the feeling of distance and scale that I
initially associated with Shiva.

The combination of monumental spaces, cold materials and large amounts of
empty space can support a visual direction based around power, isolation,
elegance and decay.

These elements may provide useful references for the future environment,
architecture and web design of the project.

---

## 5. Character Recognition

Shiva remains recognizable through a combination of recurring rather than
single defining elements.

The strongest visual anchors identified through the research are:

- Pale blue skin
- White hair
- Ice-related visual language
- Feminine appearance
- Crown or ornamental headpiece
- Elegant and powerful presence
- Blue and white color relationships

The two FFXIV interpretations demonstrate that these elements can be
rearranged significantly without losing the character's identity.

### Personal Observations

This suggests that the project does not need to reproduce Shiva's complete
original appearance.

Instead, a smaller number of strong visual anchors can be preserved while
other elements are redesigned around the new concept.

---

## 6. Takeaways for the Project

The contextual research reinforced the flexibility already identified during
the lore research.

Shiva has been visually reinterpreted many times, and even FFXIV presents
two significantly different versions of her. This demonstrates that her
identity can survive substantial changes in clothing, proportions, styling
and visual emphasis.

For the project, the main recognizable anchors will therefore be more
important than reproducing the complete original design.

The combination of pale blue, white, ice-like forms, long hair and
crown-like elements provides a strong visual foundation that can be adapted
into a new identity.

The research also supports my initial visual interpretation of Shiva as a
powerful, distant and refined figure. At the same time, the lore research
introduced themes of love, sacrifice and tragedy that can provide additional
depth if they become relevant to the final concept.

The next stage will be to determine how these visual and narrative
characteristics can be transformed into an original concept rather than
simply reproduced from the game.
