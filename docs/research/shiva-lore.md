# Lore Research — Shiva

## 1. Overview

Shiva is a recurring figure throughout the Final Fantasy series. Although her
role and story change between games, she is repeatedly reinterpreted in
different ways rather than following one fixed characterization.

This makes Shiva particularly interesting for this project: her identity can
be adapted into different narratives while still retaining recognizable
characteristics.

## 2. Recurring Identity

Across different Final Fantasy interpretations, several characteristics
appear repeatedly:

- Blue skin
- Ice and cold
- Beauty
- Strength
- Serenity
- Dignity
- Emotional intensity

Her visual identity has remained more consistent than her personality or
narrative. The association between Shiva, ice and a blue-skinned woman is one
of the strongest recurring elements across the series.

## 3. Recurring Themes

Shiva's stories vary significantly, but several themes appear repeatedly:

- Love
- Sacrifice
- Tragedy
- Strength
- Transformation
- Emotional attachment
- Beauty and suffering

These themes are not present in exactly the same way in every version, but
their recurrence shows how flexible Shiva's character can be when placed in
different narratives.

## 4. Reinterpretation Potential

One of the main discoveries from this research is that Shiva has already been
reinterpreted many times throughout the Final Fantasy series.

Because there is no single version that completely defines her personality or
story, the character provides room for a new interpretation without requiring
a direct reproduction of an existing narrative.

For this project, the FFXIV version will remain the primary visual anchor.
Recognizable elements from her FFXIV appearance can preserve her identity for
FFXIV players while allowing the surrounding concept, setting and narrative
to be developed independently.

## 5. Takeaways for the Project

Before researching Shiva's lore, my visual interpretation of her was based
almost entirely on her appearance. I initially perceived her as distant,
powerful, refined and authoritative, with characteristics that suggested
nobility, monarchy and an almost divine presence.

The lore research revealed a different side of the character. Across her
different interpretations, Shiva is frequently associated with love,
sacrifice, tragedy and emotional attachment. These characteristics were not
something I could have understood from the FFXIV visual design alone.

Rather than replacing my initial interpretation, this research provides
additional material that can be used to develop it further.

The recurring reinterpretations of Shiva also create room to imagine an
original context, character development and visual narrative for the project.
These ideas can later support the development of environments, illustrations
and concept art while keeping the FFXIV appearance as the main recognizable
anchor.
