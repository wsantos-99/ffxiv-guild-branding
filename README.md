# FFXIV Guild Branding

> A multidisciplinary creative project that transforms the discovery of **Final Fantasy XIV** into the development of a fictional guild, combining research, concept development, art direction, visual identity, illustration, web design and development into a single end-to-end project.

## Project Context

I started playing Final Fantasy XIV approximately two weeks before beginning this project.

I had never played a Final Fantasy game before and had very little knowledge of its characters, world or lore.

After becoming interested in the game, I decided to use it as the foundation for a larger portfolio project — not simply creating an illustration or a logo, but developing a complete creative project from its initial concept through to its digital implementation.

Because I was still discovering the game, the project began from a largely **visual perspective**. The characters were initially approached through their appearance, design language and visual potential rather than through prior knowledge of their stories.

This became part of the project's methodology: observing, researching and interpreting a world that was still unfamiliar to me, then using those discoveries to develop the project further.

## Project Objective

The objective is to develop a complete fictional FFXIV guild project while exploring the full creative process behind it.

The project brings together:

* Research
* Character and visual analysis
* Concept development
* Creative direction
* Brand identity
* Illustration
* Web design
* Front-end development

Rather than treating these as separate exercises, the project connects them as stages of the same creative process.

## Project Goals

The project aims to:

- Demonstrate creative direction and project development from initial research to final execution.
- Explore concept development and creative problem-solving rather than focusing only on final visual outcomes.
- Demonstrate the ability to reinterpret an existing character into an original visual and conceptual direction.
- Develop a coherent visual identity from a conceptual foundation.
- Connect illustration, branding, UX/UI, web design and front-end development within a single project.
- Demonstrate adaptability across different creative and digital applications.
- Document a complete multidisciplinary design process from research and exploration to final implementation.
